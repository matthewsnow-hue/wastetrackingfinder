Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-29.

Who we are

Waste Tracking Finder is run by Matthew Snow trading as Sno Automations, a UK sole trader. Business address: The Bristol Office, 2nd Floor, 5 High Street, Westbury-on-Trym, Bristol BS9 3BY. For UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), Matthew Snow is the data controller. Contact: hello@wastetrackingfinder.co.uk. Matthew Snow is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number ZC276141. You can check this on the ICO register.

Browsing the site

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and this site sets no cookies of its own. The search and filters run in your browser. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in running a secure, working website.

Businesses we list

We list products and providers from GOV.UK's public list. A few providers may be sole traders, so a listing can include a person's trading name. We use this public business information on the basis of legitimate interests in helping waste businesses find software. To correct or query your listing, contact us.

If you email us

We use your email address and message to reply, on the basis of legitimate interests, and keep correspondence normally no more than two years.

Sponsors and payments

If you buy a sponsored listing, payment is taken by Stripe, which collects your card details directly. We never see your full card number. We use your name, business name, email, billing address and payment status to provide the listing and keep accounts (lawful basis: contract and legal obligation). Payment and accounting records are kept for at least 5 years after the 31 January deadline for the tax year they relate to, which is GOV.UK's guidance for sole traders (GOV.UK). See Stripe's privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time. Contact hello@wastetrackingfinder.co.uk. You can also complain to the ICO.

Newsletter

If you sign up, we use your email address to send the newsletter, on the basis of your consent. That means short emails when GOV.UK's software list changes and before key dates. Sign-up uses double opt-in, so you confirm by email first.

Sign-ups and our mailing list are managed by Kit (Kit, Inc.), a US-based email service that processes the data on our behalf (see its data processing addendum). Transfers of UK personal data to the US are protected by the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. Kit may record whether you open or click our emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email hello@wastetrackingfinder.co.uk to unsubscribe. When you unsubscribe, we delete your address, or keep only a suppression record so we don't email you again. See Kit's privacy policy.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It sets no cookies and doesn't store IP addresses or build profiles of visitors. It tells us totals such as which pages are read and which sites sent visitors. GoatCounter is run from Ireland and stores its data in Finland and Germany. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in understanding how the site is used. See GoatCounter's privacy notes.
