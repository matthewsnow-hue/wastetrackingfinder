Wastrio vs Checkwright

Both products are on GOV.UK's list of software providers for the Report receipt of waste service. This page puts our dataset facts next to each other. It does not say which is better.

Why these two: Wastrio (254 impressions in Search Console, 23 Oct to 19 Nov 2026) and Checkwright are both in our general waste management group and both have a hand-checked website. The column order is not a ranking. No payment or sponsorship affects what is shown here.

What each vendor's own site says

Wastrio

  • Its page title calls it “Digital Waste Tracking Software for Permitted Waste Receiving Sites”.
  • Its page description says it is “compatible with Digital Waste Tracking, Report Receipt of Waste”. The rest of the page loads by script and was not readable in our check.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

Checkwright

  • Says it is built for UK waste carriers, skip hire operators and receiving sites.
  • Says it has live production access for Phase 1 submissions, and advertises a 14-day free trial, no contracts and no setup fees.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

Questions to ask both providers

  • Does it submit to the Report receipt of waste service for your site type, and is that included in the price?
  • What does it cost for your number of sites, users or vehicles, and is VAT extra?
  • Can you try it first, and what support do you get?
