SkipM8 vs Skip Tracker

Both products are on GOV.UK's list of software providers for the Report receipt of waste service. This page puts our dataset facts next to each other. It does not say which is better.

Why these two: Both are in our skip hire group and both have a hand-checked website. The column order is not a ranking. No payment or sponsorship affects what is shown here.

What each vendor's own site says

SkipM8

  • Says it covers bookings, dispatch, waste transfer notes, DEFRA reporting and invoicing in one system for skip hire and waste operators.
  • Says invoices can go to Xero, QuickBooks, Sage or FreeAgent.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

Skip Tracker

  • Describes itself as a skip hire management system and shows “DEFRA Digital Waste Tracking Ready!” on its homepage. That is the vendor's claim; we have not verified it.
  • Advertises £49 + VAT a month for up to 10 users and a free 7-day trial.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

Questions to ask both providers

  • Does it submit to the Report receipt of waste service for your site type, and is that included in the price?
  • What does it cost for your number of sites, users or vehicles, and is VAT extra?
  • Can you try it first, and what support do you get?

GOV.UK's guidance: Report receipt of waste, and check if you need to report the waste you receive. The provider list is here.
