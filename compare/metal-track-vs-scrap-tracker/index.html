Metal Track vs Scrap Tracker

Both products are on GOV.UK's list of software providers for the Report receipt of waste service. This page puts our dataset facts next to each other. It does not say which is better.

Why these two: Both are in our scrap metal group and have similar names, so people often mix them up. Both have a hand-checked website. The column order is not a ranking. No payment or sponsorship affects what is shown here.

What each vendor's own site says

Metal Track

  • Describes itself as scrap metal management software for dealers, and says it is “Defra approved”. That is the vendor's claim; we have not verified it.
  • Lists “Automated Submissions to DEFRA and SEPA” under digital waste tracking.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

Scrap Tracker

  • Describes itself as a scrap business system covering purchases, self bills, sales, payments and weighbridge tickets, and advertises a free 7-day trial.
  • Says it can prepare structured waste movement records to support digital submissions.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

Questions to ask both providers

  • Does it submit to the Report receipt of waste service for your site type, and is that included in the price?
  • What does it cost for your number of sites, users or vehicles, and is VAT extra?
  • Can you try it first, and what support do you get?

GOV.UK's guidance: Report receipt of waste, and check if you need to report the waste you receive. The provider list is here.
