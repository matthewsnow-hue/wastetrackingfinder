Weighsoft vs OpenWeigh

Both products are on GOV.UK's list of software providers for the Report receipt of waste service. This page puts our dataset facts next to each other. It does not say which is better.

Why these two: Both are in our weighbridges and weighing group, and both have a hand-checked website. Weighsoft had 132 Search Console impressions in the same period. The column order is not a ranking. No payment or sponsorship affects what is shown here.

What each vendor's own site says

Weighsoft

  • The Access Group's Isys page says Weighsoft and Roundsman are the Isys products, now under The Access Group.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

OpenWeigh

  • Describes itself as weighbridge ticketing software, and advertises a 7-day free trial.
  • Lists customer, product and vehicle databases, and EWC code and waste carrier licence storage.

Source: vendor's own page, read 22 Nov 2026. Vendor claims, not our checks.

Questions to ask both providers

  • Does it submit to the Report receipt of waste service for your site type, and is that included in the price?
  • What does it cost for your number of sites, users or vehicles, and is VAT extra?
  • Can you try it first, and what support do you get?
